Privacy Policy

Image Reel Randomizer (Android app, package com.imagereelrandomizer.app)
Effective date: November 6, 2026

The short version
  • Your photos, names, draws and any custom background photo stay on your device. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • Background removal runs on your device.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics of our own.
  • The Free plan shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure ads. Paid plans have no ads.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play and confirmed by RevenueCat. We never see your payment details.

1. Who we are

Image Reel Randomizer is a random picker for classes, events and presentations, made by an independent developer. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean the developer of the app. You can reach us at raffleforgeph@gmail.com.

2. Information you add to the app

You may add photos or images, names for those images, draw titles, an optional photo to use as the app background (Custom theme), and settings such as theme, language and spin length.

  • Where it is kept: only in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Who can see it: only you, on your device. We do not receive, upload, store or share it.
  • Photo access: the app only reads the images you choose in your device's picker. It does not scan your gallery and does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or location.
  • Background removal: the app uses an on-device model (Google MediaPipe) to cut people out of photos. The processing happens on your device and your photos are not sent anywhere. The model is built into the app. If it cannot load, the app may download the model files from Google (storage.googleapis.com) or the jsDelivr network. Those requests contain only the standard technical details any download contains, such as your IP address, and never your photos.

3. Advertising (Free plan only)

On the Free plan the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID,
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location,
  • interactions with ads and the app, such as app launches, taps and ad views,
  • diagnostic information, such as performance data.

Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent message. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings > Privacy policy > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Upgrading to any paid plan (Basic, Pro or Business) removes all ads, and the ad SDK then stops showing ads. Learn more about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Purchases

Paid plans are one-time purchases made through Google Play. Google Play handles your payment. We never receive your card number or other payment details.

To confirm and restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID created by the app, your purchase history (which plan you bought, when, and the purchase token), and basic device and app details such as OS version, app version, locale and currency. RevenueCat uses this only to confirm your purchases and keep your plan unlocked. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not run our own servers, analytics or tracking.
  • We do not ask for your name, email or phone number, and there is no account.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your device: remove images inside the app, or clear the app's data or uninstall it to delete everything it stored.
  • Purchase records: email us and we will delete your record from RevenueCat. Your Google Play order history is managed by Google in your Google account.
  • Advertising data: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, and manage Google ad data at myadcenter.google.com.

8. Security

Data sent by the ad and purchase services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Data the app stores on your device is kept in its private storage, which other apps cannot read on a normally configured Android device.

9. Children

Image Reel Randomizer is a tool for teachers, presenters and event hosts, and it is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe, or privacy laws in the Philippines and elsewhere), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, and to object to or limit some processing. Because we hold no account data, most requests are handled by the steps in section 7. For anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Important changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes on Google Play.
